About S.S. Plumbing & Heating

Family-owned and operated in Winnipeg since 1963.

Service &
Satisfaction.

SS stands for Service & Satisfaction — and we've been proving it to Winnipeg since 1963.

S.S. Plumbing & Heating Co. Ltd. is a family-owned, full-service plumbing, heating, cooling and electrical contractor. For more than 60 years we have handled renovations and repairs for homes and businesses across Winnipeg and Manitoba.

Why deal with three different contractors when one call brings in the experts who know this city best? Our plumbers, HVAC technicians and electricians work as one team, so your project is planned and finished together.

  • Family-owned and operated since 1963
  • Residential & commercial renovations and repairs
  • Plumbing, heating & cooling, and electrical under one roof
  • 24/7 emergency service
  • COR certified (CSAM) safety program
